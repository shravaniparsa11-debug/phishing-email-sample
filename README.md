# phishing-email-sample
Description
This project focuses on analyzing a sample phishing email to identify common phishing indicators such as suspicious sender addresses, urgent language, threatening messages, suspicious links, and requests for personal information. The analysis also explains basic email header information and safe actions to take when a suspicious email is received.
Objective
To analyze a sample phishing email and identify common phishing indicators such as suspicious sender addresses, urgent messages, suspicious links, and requests for personal information.
Phishing Email sample
From: security-alert@example-support.com
To: user@example.com
Subject: Urgent: Your Account Will Be Suspended

Dear Customer,

We detected unusual activity on your account.
Your account will be suspended within 24 hours unless
you verify your information immediately.

Please click the verification link and confirm your
account details.

Regards,
Account Security Team
Phishing Indicators
🚨 Urgent language – “within 24 hours”
⚠️ Threatening message – account suspension
📧 Suspicious sender address
🔗 Suspicious verification link
🔐 Request for account information
🎭 Social engineering
Email Header Analysis

Finding:Not available
Suspicious Link Analysis
The email asks the user to click a verification link. This is suspicious because phishing emails often use fake links to redirect users to fraudulent websites and collect sensitive information.
Action Taken: The suspicious link was not clicked.
 Conclusion
The email shows several common phishing indicators, including a suspicious sender address, urgent language, threatening messages, and a request to click a verification link. Therefore, the email should be treated as suspicious. Users should avoid clicking unknown links or sharing personal information.
 Outcome
I learned how to identify common phishing indicators and analyze suspicious emails safely.
